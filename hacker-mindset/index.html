Hacker mindset

  2. 02

    Insider access

    Premium access? How about insider access? Someone needs to be shown first to the new users anyway. Another win-win-win for everyone involved.

  3. 03

    Social media as dating infrastructure

    A tactful, respectful, professional intro message. A tiny bit of small talk to show cultural awareness. Then a direct compliment and sorting out logistics. A genuine, honest, refreshing approach that adds value and boosts confidence.
